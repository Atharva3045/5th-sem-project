How do I get started?+

Choose a language, explore this page, and try the symptom demo below. You don’t need an account.

Which signs need immediate medical attention?+

Seek immediate medical care for warning signs such as bleeding, severe abdominal pain, or severe headache during pregnancy. This is not a complete list. Do not wait for a demo response.

See help options
Does Vyana replace a doctor?+

No. This is a support-tool prototype. It does not provide a diagnosis, treatment, or emergency dispatch.

Can I use this offline today?+

Offline downloads and synchronization are not implemented in this version. Saved guidance is planned for a later phase. Calls and alerts cannot be assumed to work without connectivity.
